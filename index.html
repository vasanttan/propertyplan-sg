📖 How to use PropertyPlan SG

A Singapore property purchase calculator for working out what an HDB flat or private condo will really cost you — stamp duties (BSD and ABSD), down payment, monthly loan payments, TDSR and CPF usage — and for comparing options side by side before you commit. Everything runs in your browser: no sign-up, and nothing is sent to a server.

1

Enter your profile

Age (your loan must end by 65, which caps the tenure), household income, CPF available and monthly CPF contribution, and buyer profile (Citizen, PR or Foreigner; 1st, 2nd or 3rd property) — this sets your ABSD.

2

Enter the property

Property type (HDB or condo), price, down payment (at least 25% for a resale condo), loan tenure (up to 30 years) and interest rate (HDB loan 2.6%; bank loans typically 2.6–3.5%).

3

Calculate and review

Click Calculate Payment Plan to see your monthly payment, TDSR (limit 55%), stamp duties, total cash needed upfront, the payment chart and the month-by-month schedule (exportable to CSV).

4

Save and compare

Click 💾 Save Scenario for each option you try, then 📊 Compare Scenarios to put 2–10 side by side. 🏆 marks the best value on each metric; switch between Classic 🎴 and Compact 📋 views or export to CSV.

5

Check the HDB resale market

Scroll to HDB Resale Medians and drag the budget slider to see which flat types and towns fall within your budget, based on HDB's 2026 median resale prices.

Questions it helps you answer

  • What can I afford? Compare an HDB flat against a condo by monthly payment and cash needed.
  • 25% or 30% down? Trade a bigger upfront payment for lower monthly payments and less interest.
  • 20- or 30-year loan? Shorter costs more each month but far less interest overall.
  • Can I upgrade? Model your next property with 2nd-property ABSD and see if it passes TDSR.
  • How does my age limit my loan? See the maximum tenure applied automatically.

For estimation and planning only — not financial advice. Rates reflect regulations as of January 2025 and may change; confirm figures with your bank, HDB, IRAS or a licensed advisor before committing.
